Privacy Policy

Drama Lingo: Learn Korean · Effective October 1, 2026

SupportTerms of Use

Short version: Drama Lingo does not collect, store, sell or share any personal data. There are no accounts, no analytics and no ads.

What stays on your device

Your episode progress, saved words, review schedule, streak and settings are stored only on your device (and on your paired Apple Watch, which syncs directly with your iPhone). We never receive them. Deleting the app deletes this data.

Speech and Apple Intelligence

Korean audio is generated on your device by Apple's built-in speech voices. Optional "Explain" and "Quiz me" features use Apple's on-device Foundation Models when Apple Intelligence is available on your device; this processing is performed by Apple's frameworks and is not sent to us. The optional translation sheet uses Apple's Translation framework.

Purchases

Subscriptions are processed by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive your payment details or identity; the app only receives a confirmation from Apple that your subscription is active.

Episode updates

The app may download an updated episode file from this website (GitHub Pages). This request contains no personal information; like any web request, GitHub's servers may log standard technical data such as IP address under GitHub's privacy statement. We do not access or use those logs.

Notifications

If you turn on daily reminders, they are scheduled locally on your device. No push server is involved.

Children

Drama Lingo does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date. Questions: open a support request.
