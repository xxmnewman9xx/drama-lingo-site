FAQ

How do I restore my subscription?
Open the app → Profile → Restore Purchases. Purchases are tied to your Apple Account.

How do I cancel?
Settings app → your name → Subscriptions → Drama Lingo, or Profile → Manage Subscription in the app. Cancel at least 24 hours before renewal.

The Korean voice sounds robotic.
Drama Lingo uses the voices on your device. For a more natural voice, go to Settings → Accessibility → Spoken Content → Voices → Korean and download an Enhanced or Premium voice.

Which episodes are free?
Episodes 1–3 are free. Drama Lingo Pro unlocks episodes 4–30. Review, Apple Watch, widgets and Apple Intelligence explanations are free for everyone.

Do I need an account?
No. Your progress stays on your device.
